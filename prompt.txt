Build a real-time, realistic-but-painterly scene of the land around Madinah at golden hour that runs in the
browser. It only needs to look beautiful and let me walk (and, with a key, fly) through it.

PRIORITIES (in this order when they conflict)
1. Visual quality
2. Stable frame pacing at the target spec
3. Small bundle size

CONSTRAINTS
- No Three.js/Babylon.js or other engines.
- WebGPU rendered.
- Everything procedural: no texture/model/audio files. Deliverable is a single self-contained index.html.
  Report minified size.
- Zero per-frame JS allocations (no GC stutter). Preallocate all buffers.
- No human figures anywhere. No interior of the Prophet's chamber. No synthesized adhan or Qur'an recitation.
  Buildings are respectful close approximations of the real ones, not replicas; geography is composed and
  compressed, not to scale.

TARGET
- Omarchy Linux, Chrome 157, RTX 3070, 60 Hz+ monitor
- 2560x1440 at full resolution. GPU frame time <= 11 ms average, p99 <= 16 ms during a scripted 60 s
  fly-through.

SCENE
- A date-palm grove like the orchards of Quba and Awali: tall palms with heavy, swaying fronds and orange-gold
  date clusters, mud-brick garden walls, an irrigation channel with still water, a stone well
- Warm desert air: clear near, a soft golden-to-blue haze with distance, long shadows from a low western sun
- Light: sun shafts through palm fronds and dust, a warm sky fading to blue overhead with thin high cloud
- Landforms: the black basalt edge of the harrat lava field (blocky, cracked, rough), a sandy wadi bed with
  rounded cobbles, a low granite hill to climb, Mount Uhud as a long red-brown granite massif on the
  northern horizon, Jabal Ayr low and dark to the south
- Second zone: the black harrat plain to the east with scattered scoria cones and sparse dry shrubs
- Life: pigeon flocks circling, small birds in the grove, drifting dust motes and pollen in the light
- Set pieces: Masjid an-Nabawi on the horizon (cream walls, many tall minarets, the Green Dome in the
  south-east of the old mosque, silver roof domes, open courtyard umbrellas), and a white mosque with domes
  and four minarets in the style of Masjid Quba at the edge of the grove; a low city of cream and white
  buildings between them
- World: bounded, but the boundaries can't be visible from the starting area

CONTROLS
WASD walk, mouse look (pointer lock), Shift to run, F toggles flight (then Q/E down/up)

VERIFICATION
- Visual critic: a separate subagent that sees ONLY your renders, never your code or reasoning. Give it 6
  fixed camera poses, rendered at 2560x1440 in GPU-accelerated Chrome, plus frames sampled from the scripted
  fly-through to catch shimmer, aliasing, popping and banding. It returns prioritized, concrete defects.
  Iterate until it reports nothing above "minor".
- Performance: GPU timestamp queries plus a frame-time log from the scripted fly-through. Report average,
  99th percentile and max.

DONE WHEN: both verification gates pass. Deliver index.html, the bundle size, the perf report and the
critic's final review.
